FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about P2P FX Terminal, syncing, analytics, and privacy.

What is P2P FX Terminal? ✕

P2P FX Terminal is a trading journal that does the heavy lifting for you. It syncs your MT4/MT5 trades automatically, tracks your P&L, lets you journal and backtest, and uses AI to show you what you're doing right and wrong. It works for forex, stocks, crypto — anything you trade on MetaTrader or any broker.

How does the MT5 sync work? ✕

You connect your MT4 or MT5 account using your investor password (read-only, so it can't touch your funds). Once connected, your trades sync in real time. Every new position, every close — it all shows up automatically in your journal. No manual entry needed.

What do the AI reports actually tell me? ✕

The AI looks at your entire trade history and breaks it down — your win rate, profit factor, risk habits, emotional patterns, blind spots, and even revenge trading tendencies. It gives you a letter grade and a specific list of things to work on to get better.

How does backtesting work? ✕

You pick a symbol, timeframe, and date range, then the chart replays candle by candle. You can open and close positions as if you were trading live, complete with stop loss and take profit. At the end, you get full stats on how your strategy performed.

Is my data safe? ✕

Yes. We use bank-level encryption (AES-256) and local browser storage so your trade logs stay strictly private. We can never place, modify, or close trades on your behalf. Your data is yours — we don't share it with anyone.

Is there a free plan? ✕

Absolutely. Our community edition gives you unlimited manual trade entries, equity curve analytics, and daily P&L calendar heatmaps completely free. If you want cloud MT5 sync, AI reports, and backtesting, you can explore Pro and Elite.

Can I share my performance? ✕

Yes. You can generate share cards in different formats (story, post, landscape) and download them or share directly to social media, or export your CSV/JSON reports anytime.

What markets are supported? ✕

All markets — forex pairs, gold, silver, indices, F&O, stocks, and crypto. You can log and filter trades across every asset class in one unified terminal.
