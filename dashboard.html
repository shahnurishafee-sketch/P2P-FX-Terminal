Frequently Asked Questions

GETTING STARTED How do I connect my MT5 account?

Navigate to Profile → Accounts tab, click 'Connect Account', and enter your MT5 credentials. We use MetaAPI for secure, read-only access to your trading data.

GETTING STARTED What brokers are supported?

We support all brokers compatible with MetaAPI, which includes most major MT4/MT5 brokers worldwide. Check MetaAPI's broker list for full compatibility.

SECURITY How is my data protected?

We use bank-level encryption, never store your broker passwords, and only have read-only access to your trading data. Your data is never shared with third parties.

FEATURES Can I export my journal entries?

Yes! Go to Journal → click the export button in the top right. You can export as CSV, PDF, or JSON format for your records.

FEATURES How do I choose, move or resize columns in the trades table?

Open Trades and click View. Tick the columns you want, drag them into order, and pick a row height and page size. You can also drag a column header to move it, drag its edge to resize it, and Shift+click headers to sort by more than one column. Your layout is saved to your account and follows you to every device.

FEATURES Can I see totals for a group of trades?

Drag across cells in the trades table, or click one and Shift+click another, to see their sum, average, minimum and maximum below the table. Press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on Mac) to copy them or click Chart to plot them. Tick rows to copy them or export them to Excel or CSV.

FEATURES How do I arrange journal and trade analysis sections?

Journal entries and Trade Analysis have an Arrange button. Use it to choose which sections are visible and reorder them. Your layout is saved to your account.

BILLING What's included in the Pro plan?

Pro includes up to 3 connected MT4/MT5 accounts, unlimited trade history, advanced analytics, AI trade insights, and priority support. Upgrade to Elite for unlimited accounts and multi-account views. Cancel anytime.

BILLING How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Profile → Billing tab and click 'Manage Subscription'. You can cancel anytime and will retain access until the end of your billing period.

ACCOUNT How do I reset my password?

Click 'Forgot Password' on the login page, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox. For security, links expire after 1 hour.

TROUBLESHOOTING Why are my trades not syncing?

Ensure your investor password is read-only and active on your broker's server, or use '+ Add Trade' / CSV Import anytime for instant manual logging.
